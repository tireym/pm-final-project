# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** The ecommerce lead at a large online retailer that offers Riverty at checkout and is being pitched on the new card.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Keep Riverty as a payment option that brings her shop incremental sales and loyal customers, rather than one more interchangeable method at the checkout.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** She asks Riverty, “If your card pays at every shop, why would my customers still pick you at my checkout? Show me what I get.” (N-08). The product gives her no answer. Once a customer holds a card that works anywhere, her checkout loses its distinct reason to choose Riverty, and nothing in the current test offers her a return. Partner offers (B5) exist only as a backlog item.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** email, phone call, BI tools, spreadsheets,
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** emailing, review BI dashboards, checking spread sheets, manual before and after consumer behavior reviews
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** “Prove it ourselves, outside Riverty” how can the merchant actually see measurable change in consumer behavior to their own benefit.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** “Prove it ourselves, outside Riverty”
